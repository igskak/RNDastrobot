# GTM Execution Plan — Bootstrap → Grant → $1M ARR

Companion to [MONETIZATION_PLAN.md](MONETIZATION_PLAN.md). This is the *how we get there* plan, built around the founder's bootstrapping path: fast paid traction → Czech grant → funded growth.

Researched June 2026. Numbers tie to the benchmarked model in the monetization plan (base case: 15% trial→paid, 4% monthly churn, ~$28 blended ARPU = $336/yr).

---

## The funding logic (corrected expectations)

The founder's instinct is right: small revenue traction unlocks non-dilutive grant money, which funds growth. But one €30k grant alone does NOT get you to $1M ARR. It funds the *first scaling step* and de-risks the next raise. The realistic capital stack:

| Stage | Source | Amount | Buys |
|-------|--------|--------|------|
| Bootstrap | Founder-led sales, ~$0 spend | — | First 20 paid, validation |
| Grant 1 | Czech innovation voucher / CzechStarter | €20-40k | First growth experiments, part-time bizdev |
| Grant 2 (bigger) | **CzechInvest Technology Incubation** | up to CZK 5M (~€200k), 18-24 mo | The real growth engine |
| Optional | Pre-seed angel / Czech VC (Credo, Rockaway) or EIC | €50-200k | Accelerate to $1M if grants insufficient |

**Key finding: aim at CzechInvest Technology Incubation, not just the €20-40k voucher.** The 2025 "Incubation PLUS" call is up to CZK 5M (~€200k) over 18-24 months, explicitly for pre-seed tech startups where commercial capital is hard. AI-powered astrology session summaries qualify as the "innovative technology" angle. That single grant is ~5x your target and could fund most of the path to $1M.

**Hard prerequisite for any Czech grant: a Czech legal entity (s.r.o.).** EU grants require incorporation in an EU member state. For a Ukrainian founder this means registering a Czech s.r.o. before applying. Bonus: a Czech s.r.o. is also clean for Paddle MoR. Do this early — it gates everything downstream.

Sources: [CzechInvest Technology Incubation](https://czechinvest.gov.cz/en/Homepage/News/November-1/Another-100-million-for-innovation-Technology-Incubation-expands-support-for-Czech-startups), [CzechInvest For Startups](https://czechinvest.gov.cz/en/For-Startups), [Podporastartupu / CzechStarter](https://national-policies.eacea.ec.europa.eu/youthwiki/chapters/czechia/39-start-up-funding-for-young-entrepreneurs).

## Phase M0 — First 20 paid users (~$500 MRR), ~$0 spend

Goal is not revenue, it is **proof for the grant application and the first testimonials.** This is pure founder-led, do-things-that-don't-scale.

Tactics:
1. **Direct outreach to practicing astrologers** you can reach now: UA/RU astrology Telegram channels and communities, Instagram astrologers, personal/warm network. DM, offer a personal demo.
2. **Founding-member offer:** lifetime discount (e.g. Standard €15 locked forever) in exchange for feedback + a public testimonial. Cap at the first cohort.
3. **Manual white-glove onboarding:** personally set up each user's first client chart and watch them run one consultation. This is your activation engine and your fastest learning loop.
4. **One or two astrology-school conversations** to line up a Phase-M1 pilot.

Target: 20 paid in 4-8 weeks. At ~$25 avg that is ~$500 MRR. Instrument the funnel from day one (trial start, activation, conversion) — you will need these numbers for the grant.

Exit criteria for M0: 20 paying users, 3+ written testimonials, funnel metrics captured, Czech s.r.o. registration started.

## Phase M1 — Grant 1 + founding cohort (~$20k ARR)

Run in parallel:
- **Grant application.** Use M0 traction as evidence. Start with CzechInvest pre-incubation consultation (free) to shape the application; apply to Technology Incubation when the call opens (recurring calls; the 2025 call ran Nov 24 2025 – Jan 9 2026, so track the next window). Also pursue an innovation voucher (€5-25k) as a faster, smaller parallel bet.
- **Founding cohort to ~50-80 paid** via 2-3 school partnerships (revenue share / extended student trial) and 3-5 astrologer-influencer deals (free tool + affiliate code).

Exit criteria: grant submitted (ideally secured), ~50-80 paid, school + influencer channels validated.

---

## Phase M2 — PLG engine + referral (~$170k ARR)

Grant money deployed here. This is where "promotion/bizdev" spend goes.

Allocation of a ~€30-200k grant (priority order):
1. **Part-time growth/bizdev hire** — owns school partnerships + influencer pipeline (highest leverage, this is relationship work).
2. **Referral program launch** (built per monetization plan) — cheapest channel at ~$50 CAC.
3. **Content + organic social cadence** (IG/TikTok/YouTube Shorts/Telegram) — astrology is highly viral.
4. **Localized onboarding polish** — activation is the #1 churn lever (43% of churn in first 90 days).
5. **Small paid-ads test budget** — only once trial→paid is proven; cap at CAC ceiling (~$195, target $60-100).

Exit criteria: ~500 paid, referral driving a measurable share of new signups, repeatable school/influencer playbook, English-market beachhead content started.

## Phase M3 → M4 — Scale to $1M (~$500k → $1M ARR)

The monetization plan's TAM research is decisive here: **$1M requires winning English-speaking markets (US/UK/CA/AU), not UA/RU alone.** 3,000 paying ≈ 4-6% of the ~50-80k EN+UK+RU serviceable market — achievable only as the category-leading tool.

M3 (≈$500k): English-market entry. Localized English GTM, English astrology influencers/communities, content/SEO in English. Layer paid ads on the proven funnel. This is likely where a pre-seed angel round (€50-200k from Czech VCs like Credo Ventures / Rockaway, or EIC blended finance) accelerates things if grant + revenue aren't enough.

M4 ($1M): English-market leadership + LATAM (ES/PT) language expansion as upside (cheap given the existing i18n layer). At 4% churn you are replacing ~120 customers/month, so retention/onboarding investment compounds.

## Critical-path dependencies (do these in order)
1. Register Czech s.r.o. (gates all grants + Paddle).
2. Ship the billing MVP (two tiers + reverse-trial + Paddle) so you can actually charge the first 20.
3. Get 20 paid (M0) — proof.
4. Apply for grant(s) using that proof.
5. Deploy grant into the PLG + referral engine.
6. Pivot acquisition to English markets for the back half to $1M.

## Locked constraints (founder decisions)
- **Timeline: 24 months to $1M ARR.**
- **Czech s.r.o.: not a blocker** — incorporate now (step 1, gates grants + Paddle).
- **Small team + part-time bizdev hire** (the M2 growth role).
- **Funding: grants + revenue only, no dilution.**

### Feasibility verdict (honest)
24 months to $1M, grant+revenue-only, is aggressive-but-doable IF three things hold: (1) base-case conversion (15%) or better, (2) the English-market entry in the back half works, (3) you reinvest nearly all gross profit into growth during the ramp. **No-dilution means you do NOT take the ~$640k profit during these 24 months — it funds the acceleration.** If conversion lands bear-case (10%), 24 months slips toward 36. The single biggest schedule risk is grant timing (see below).

### 24-month quarterly roadmap

| Qtr | Months | Paid (end) | ARR | Milestone focus |
|-----|--------|-----------|-----|-----------------|
| Q1 | 1-3 | ~20 | ~$6k | s.r.o. registered, billing MVP shipped, M0 founder-led sales |
| Q2 | 4-6 | ~80 | ~$20k | Grant 1 applied, founding cohort, school pilots, **part-time bizdev hired** |
| Q3 | 7-9 | ~250 | ~$70k | Grant 1 in, referral live, PLG cadence |
| Q4 | 10-12 | ~500 | ~$170k | Technology Incubation grant secured, partnerships repeatable |
| Q5 | 13-15 | ~900 | ~$300k | English-market entry begins |
| Q6 | 16-18 | ~1,500 | ~$500k | English content/influencer engine, paid ads scaling |
| Q7 | 19-21 | ~2,200 | ~$740k | English-market leadership push |
| Q8 | 22-24 | ~3,000 | **$1M** | LATAM upside, retention/onboarding compounding |

Back-half reality: net adds must climb from ~60/mo (Q2) to ~300/mo (Q8); at 4% churn on a 3,000 base you replace ~120/mo, so peak gross adds ≈ 400/mo. That cadence is what grant+revenue must fund.

### Grant timing is critical-path (act now)
Technology Incubation calls are roughly annual (2025 call: Nov 24 2025 – Jan 9 2026). For a 24-month plan you likely get ONE good shot at the big grant — missing the window costs ~12 months. Mitigation: start CzechInvest **pre-incubation consultation immediately** (free, shapes the application), and run a faster innovation voucher (€5-25k) in parallel so you are not blocked waiting for the annual call.

### Part-time bizdev hire (Q2)
Hire when you have the founding cohort + school pilots to hand off (M1→M2 boundary). Role owns school partnerships + influencer pipeline — relationship work that does not scale via ads. Fund from grant 1. This frees the founder for product + grant 2 + English-market strategy.

### What to watch
- If Q3-Q4 conversion is tracking <12%, the 24-month line is at risk — decide then whether to reconsider a small angel round (you prefer not to, but it is the release valve).
- English-market entry (Q5) is the hinge. If it underperforms, $1M on UA/RU alone is not reachable (per TAM research). De-risk by starting English content in Q4, before you need it.
