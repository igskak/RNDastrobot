What software do professional astrologers use?

A short, factual answer with the programs named, their platforms, their prices, and what each one leaves out.

Most working astrologers use one of a handful of programs: Solar Fire on Windows, Astro Gold on Mac and mobile, TimePassages, Chronos in the Russian-speaking world, or a browser workspace such as Astrolium, Astrologer or Steliara.

There is no industry survey behind that list, and anyone who tells you there is probably has a product to sell. What there is: a small set of programs that come up again and again in professional practice, split neatly into two kinds. One kind calculates charts very deeply and knows nothing about your week. The other kind tries to hold the practice as well.

The split that actually matters

Read that table again and you can see the line. Solar Fire, Astro Gold and TimePassages are calculators: they compute more than most astrologers will ever use, they run on one machine, and the most they keep of a reading is whatever you found time to type into a comment field afterwards. Astrolium, Astrologer, Chronos and Steliara run in a browser and try to hold more of the practice.

Which means the real question is not "what do professionals use". It is "what part of your week is the painful part". If the painful part is a chart technique you cannot get elsewhere, buy the deepest calculator. If it is the scheduling, buy the one built around booking. If it is that you finish a two-hour reading and six months later you are the one working from memory, that is a different tool again.

Where Steliara sits, honestly

Steliara is one of the browser workspaces, and it would be useless to pretend it is the widest or the cheapest. It is not. What it has instead is one thing the others' own pages do not describe: the consultation. That is the whole reason to pick it, and if it does not matter to you, one of the other programs above is a better buy.

AI will never read a chart for you. It just finds the facts faster: the aspects, the exact dates, where a planet sits. The summary captures what you said. You stay the astrologer.

Questions

Is Solar Fire still the professional standard?

For calculation depth on Windows it is still the reference point. Solar Fire V9 costs $360 for a new license, lists 30 house systems, interpretation reports, full mapping and Vedic dasas, and its maker states it runs on Microsoft-supported versions of Windows including Windows 8, 10 and 11. It does organize charts into client files with unlimited comments per chart. What it does not do is record a consultation. Checked on alabe.com on 12 October 2026.

Can professional astrologers work on a Mac?

Yes, in two ways. Astro Gold ships a macOS version, and the browser workspaces run on any machine. Solar Fire is the one that does not: its maker's own support page points Mac users at running Windows programs under emulation. That is the usual reason astrologers go looking for a Solar Fire alternative.

Do professional astrologers use free software?

Many use free sites such as Astro-Seek or Astrodienst for a quick chart, and Astrolium publishes a free tier with all 23 of its house systems and five client profiles. Free tools are fine for calculating a chart. They stop being enough once a practice needs the history of everyone you read for in one place.

Which astrology software records and transcribes a consultation?

Of the programs named here, Steliara is the one that does. It runs the consultation as a video call, records the audio once both sides agree, transcribes it and saves a short summary on the profile of the person you read for. On 12 October 2026 the public pricing and feature pages of Astrolium, Astrologer, Chronos, TimePassages and Solar Fire did not mention recording or transcribing a session.

Can I move my charts from one program to another?

Often, yes. Steliara imports chart files from ZET, Astro.com, Astrolog, and the .SFcht files that Solar Fire and Astro Gold both use, up to 5 MiB and 2,000 records per file, with possible duplicates flagged. Notes on a source chart come across for every format except Astrolog. Subsidiary charts inside a Solar Fire record and the original program's calculation settings do not.
