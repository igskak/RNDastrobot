What is the best astrology software for Windows?

What installs on a PC, what runs in a browser, and how each one is licensed and priced.

On Windows, Solar Fire X, Astro Gold and TimePassages run as installed programs, and Astrologer ships a Windows app. Astrolium and Steliara run in any browser. The real choice is a licence tied to a computer, or an account that follows you.

Windows is where the serious desktop astrology programs started. Solar Fire has been a Windows program since 1992, and for most of that time a Windows PC was simply the machine you needed. Today the question is different. You can still install a deep calculator and own it, but you can also keep your charts and the people you read for in an account that opens on any computer, and which one suits you comes down to how you work rather than which operating system you have.

Check your Windows before you buy

The newest installers are fussier than they used to be. Solar Fire X needs a 64-bit edition of Windows 10 or 11, and Astrolabe says outright that Windows 7, Windows 8, Windows 8.1 and any 32-bit Windows cannot run the current installer. It suggests you open Start, then Settings, System and About, and read your Windows edition and the System type line under Device specifications before you pay. That takes a minute and saves a refund request.

Memory and disk matter less than the version. Astrolabe recommends 8 GB of RAM and about 3 GB of free space for Solar Fire X, and says these are recommendations rather than tested minimums. Astro Gold states 8 GB of RAM as its minimum on Windows. TimePassages lists 256 MB of RAM. The practical rule is the version: a PC stuck on Windows 7 will not run the newest Solar Fire at all.

Solar Fire 9 is the exception. It is no longer sold, but the Astrolabe page for it says Windows 8, 10 and 11 all work, so owners of an existing licence are not forced to move. Whether to buy into something that is no longer sold is a different matter, and the page you would buy from is the one for Solar Fire X.

One licence, how many computers

This is the part people discover after buying. A Solar Fire X licence permits use on up to two computers, and Astrolabe is clear that uninstalling the program from an old machine does not automatically free the activation. If you replace a PC and have already used both, you write to support. The program also needs an internet connection to activate and to download updates, even though it calculates charts offline afterwards.

Astro Gold went the other way. Its Windows page says the Windows and macOS versions now share a single dual-platform licence: buy either one and the same licence covers a Windows PC and a Mac, or both. It stresses that this is one licence, not a second copy free.

A browser tool removes the question. The licence is an account, so a new PC, a borrowed laptop and a work computer all open the same charts. What you give up is the machine you own outright, which for some astrologers is the whole point.

What a browser tool saves you on Windows

There is no installer to run and no Windows version to check. Updates happen on the server, so you are never a version behind a colleague. Replacing your PC means signing in, not moving a chart database across. For an astrologer who reads on a laptop at home, a desktop at the office and sometimes a tablet, that is a real saving, and the same account is open on all three.

The cost is the mirror image. A browser tool needs a connection and does not work on a train with no signal, and the depth of technique varies a lot. Astrolium and Astrologer lean towards breadth of techniques and a practice side. Steliara leans towards the practice side only.

Where Steliara sits, honestly

Steliara is a browser workspace, so on Windows it is a tab rather than a program. It is not the widest option and it is not the cheapest. It has six house systems against the 30 that Solar Fire 9 listed, no astrocartography, no Vedic dashas, no offline mode and no free tier, and if any of those matter most to you, one of the programs above is the better choice.

What it adds is the side of the work these programs leave to other tools. Every person you read for has a profile that keeps their notes, charts and past sessions together, and the consultation itself can be run as a video call in the app, with the audio recorded once both sides agree, transcribed and summarized. Recording, transcripts and summaries are on the Studio plan.

AI will never read a chart for you. It just finds the facts faster: the aspects, the exact dates, where a planet sits. The summary captures what you said. You stay the astrologer.

Questions

What is the best astrology software for Windows?

There is no single best. If you want a calculator you own, install on your PC and use offline, Solar Fire X, Astro Gold or TimePassages are the native Windows choices. If you want the people you read for and the sessions in the same place as the charts, a browser workspace such as Astrolium, Astrologer or Steliara fits better, and works the same on a Windows laptop as on a Mac.

Does Solar Fire run on Windows 11?

Yes. Solar Fire X needs Windows 10 (64-bit) or Windows 11 on an Intel or AMD processor, and Astrolabe says Windows 7, 8, 8.1 and 32-bit Windows cannot run its installer. Solar Fire 9, which is no longer sold, is described on the Astrolabe site as working on Windows 11 too.

Can I use one licence on a Windows PC and a Mac?

It depends on the program. Astro Gold sells a single dual-platform licence that covers a Windows PC and a Mac. A Solar Fire X licence permits up to two computers, Windows or Mac, and uninstalling a copy does not automatically free an activation, so Astrolabe asks you to contact support when you replace a machine. A browser tool uses an account, so the number of computers is not part of the licence.

Is there free astrology software for Windows?

Astrolium publishes a free plan that lists Mac, PC and tablet, with five client profiles and unlimited natal charts, on its pricing page as of 3 November 2026. The desktop programs here are paid. Steliara has no free tier, but every account gets a 14-day trial of every feature with no card.

Do I need an internet connection?

For the installed programs you need one to activate Solar Fire X and to download updates, and after that they calculate charts on your own computer. A browser tool such as Steliara needs a connection whenever you use it, and has no offline mode.

Is a browser app as good as an installed one on Windows?

For calculation depth, an installed program such as Solar Fire X usually offers more options. A browser workspace gives you the same account on any computer and keeps your records in one place. Steliara is one of those: it has six house systems, no astrocartography and no Vedic dashas.
