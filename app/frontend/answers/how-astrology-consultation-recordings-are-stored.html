How are astrology consultation recordings stored?

What is recorded, who has to agree, how long the audio is kept and what happens to it afterwards.

Steliara records audio only, never video, and only after both people agree in the call. The audio is kept for six months, then deleted automatically. The transcript and summary stay on the person's profile. Recording is part of the Studio plan.

Recording is the part of a practice workspace that raises the first honest question: who holds this, and may I make it at all? The answers below come from the product itself, from its code and from its terms and privacy policy, and where a point is a question of law and not of product, the page says so and stops.

What is recorded, and when

Only the audio of the consultation. The session can be a video call inside the app, but nothing from the video is recorded, and the notice in the call says so in those words.

Recording does not begin when the call does. The astrologer is asked, and the person on the other side is asked on their own join page, where the notice tells them what will happen to the recording. Both have to say yes. This is not only a button on the screen: the server stores each agreement against the session and refuses to start a recording until both are there. If either side declines, nothing is recorded.

Agreements are stored per session, so each consultation is asked again.

What the person is told

The notice the person sees before agreeing says that only audio will be recorded and no video, that the recording will be used to prepare a summary, and that the audio is kept for six months and then deleted automatically. The astrologer sees the same facts when they request consent: the recording is stored in the person's profile and transcribed automatically, both sides must agree before it begins, the audio goes after six months, and the transcript stays in the profile.

How long it is kept, and how the deletion works

Audio is kept for six months, which the code counts as 180 days from when the recording started. The terms and privacy policy say the same in words: audio recordings are kept for six months from the day they are made and then deleted automatically.

A deletion job runs when the service starts and then once a day. It removes the audio file from storage first and only afterwards marks the recording as deleted, so a file that fails to delete is not reported as gone and is tried again on the next pass.

The transcript and the summary are a different matter. They are why the recording existed, so they stay on the person's profile until you delete them or close your account. After six months the voice is gone and the written record remains.

The date to know

The terms promise six months from the day each recording is made. The automatic deletion job was introduced on 26 September 2026, and it applies to recordings made on or after that date; it does not touch recordings made before it. We checked this page against the live terms and against the deletion job in the code on 3 November 2026, and we state it as it is, not as it would sound better.

Who is responsible for what

The terms say you keep ownership of what you add to Steliara, including recordings. They also say you are responsible for having a lawful basis to store information about the people you work with, and for informing them as applicable law requires. The privacy policy describes Steliara as the controller of account data and as a processor of the data you store about the people you work with, which is why that responsibility sits with you.

Asking in the call is not a substitute for that responsibility. It is a good habit and a built-in one, and it still leaves your own obligations where they were. Steliara does not sell personal data, which the privacy policy states.

How other tools handle it

Astrologer is the one other program we found that describes recorded sessions. On 3 November 2026 its features page listed live sessions with audio and video readings that are auto-transcribed and replayable, and its pricing page listed live video sessions on the Practitioner plan. Its pages did not say how consent or retention work, so if you use it, ask.

For Solar Fire, Astro Gold, TimePassages, Astrolium and Chronos, we found no description of recording a session on their public pages when we read them: Astro Gold on 2 November 2026 and the others on 12 October 2026. That is an absence on a page, not a promise about what they will or will not ship.

Questions to ask any tool that records sessions

Is it audio only, or video too? Who has to agree before it starts, and is that checked by the software or only by a notice? How long is the audio kept, and is it deleted automatically, by whom, and how do you find out it worked? What happens to the transcript afterwards, and can you delete it? Who owns the recording? A tool that answers all five in writing is one you can explain to the people you read for.

What this page does not say

It does not say whether recording a consultation is lawful where you work, because that differs by country and by state and we are not in a position to advise you. Places differ, and some have their own rules for professional work. A short conversation with a lawyer or your professional body is worth more than anything on a software company's page.

What it does say is what Steliara does: it asks both sides every time, it records only audio, it keeps it for six months and then deletes it.

Questions

Is it safe to record astrology consultations?

That depends on two things: how the recording is handled, and whether you have the right to make it where you and the other person are. This page covers the first. In Steliara the recording is audio only, starts only after both sides agree, and is deleted automatically after six months. The second is a question about your own country and state, and this page does not answer it.

Do I need consent to record a client session?

Consent rules differ by country and by state, and we cannot tell you what yours require. What Steliara does by default is ask both sides in the call, every time, and it will not start recording until both have agreed. For what the law asks of you, check your own jurisdiction or ask a lawyer.

How long are consultation recordings kept?

Six months. The terms say audio is kept for six months from the day it is made and then deleted automatically. The deletion runs once a day and counts from when the recording started. The transcript and summary made from it stay on the person's profile until you delete them or close your account.

Does Steliara record video?

No. The consultation can run as a video call, but only the audio is recorded, and the notice in the call says so.

Can the other person say no?

Yes. They are asked on their own join page, and the server refuses to start a recording until both the astrologer and the other person have agreed. If they say no, nothing is recorded.

Who owns the recording?

You do. The terms say you keep ownership of what you add to Steliara, including recordings, and that you are responsible for having a lawful basis to keep information about the people you work with and for informing them as applicable law demands.
