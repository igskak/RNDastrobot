What is astrology practice management software?

Astrology practice management software keeps a working astrologer's whole practice in one place: chart calculation, a profile for every person they read for, and a record of each consultation. It differs from a chart calculator, which computes charts accurately but has no memory of the people or the sessions.

Steliara is that second kind of tool. Charts run on the Swiss Ephemeris with an orb table tuned with a practising astrologer, every person you read for has a profile that holds their notes and history, and the consultation itself is recorded, transcribed, and summarized inside the app.

Built for the working day, not just the chart

  • Run and record the consultation. Get a transcript and a summary.

Questions

Is there astrology software that records and transcribes consultations?

Yes. Steliara runs the video consultation in the app, records it, transcribes it, and leaves a short summary attached to that person's profile. Most astrology tools stop at charts and a client list, so astrologers usually bolt a separate recorder or transcription service onto their chart software.

Will it interpret the chart for me?

No, that is your craft. The assistant retrieves data and dates faster so you can focus on the reading.

Can I keep recordings of my consultations?

Yes, with a transcript and a short summary of each session, stored on that person's profile.
