First, the thing most pages about Astro Gold get wrong

Astro Gold is not Apple-only. On 2 November 2026 astrogold.io sold four versions: macOS, Windows, iOS and Android. The desktop app is US$249.99, and their Windows page calls that one purchase a single dual-platform licence, so it covers Mac and Windows both.

We are starting here because it matters to anyone comparing. Half the directory pages and best-of roundups still describe Astro Gold as the Mac answer for people who cannot run Solar Fire, which was the shape of the market years ago and is not the shape of it now. If you came looking for Astro Gold for Windows, the honest answer is that the makers sell it, and your existing Mac licence already includes it. That is not a reason to look at anything else, and we are not going to pretend it is.

The same correction runs the other way. Solar Fire, from the same creators, used to be the Windows-only half of the pair. Solar Fire X is now native on Mac too. So the platform argument that used to drive these comparisons has quietly gone, for both of them, and what is left is a more interesting question: what do you actually need that a chart program is not built to do?

What Astro Gold is genuinely good at

It is a serious piece of professional software and it would be dishonest to open with anything else. From its own macOS page, checked on 2 November 2026: 44 displayed points with midpoints, minor bodies, lots, stars, nodes and apsides on top, more than 25 house systems, the tropical zodiac and nine sidereal ones, and natal, transit, return, secondary progressed, arc directed, composite, harmonic and antiscia charts, all on the Swiss Ephemeris.

Then the parts a working astrologer actually reaches for: wheels, dials, bi-, tri- and quad-wheels, aspect and synastry grids, interactive interpretations on a double-click, the Chart Explorer, graphic ephemerides, Astro Sheets covering traditional technique, Firdaria and life time arcs, Astro Locality Mapping with geodetic and parans options, an ACS Atlas of more than 250,000 places, and Professional Reports as optional paid add-ons.

It is installed on your own computer, so it opens on a train with no signal. And it has real apps in the Apple and Android stores, which is something we cannot say.

If what you want is a deeper calculator, this page has nothing better to offer you. Keep it.

Astro Gold or Solar Fire, since they come from the same people

This question comes up constantly, and the usual answer, Solar Fire for Windows and Astro Gold for Mac, is no longer true of either. Both now run on Mac and Windows. Their chart files are interchangeable: astrogold.io states that Astro Gold's files are fully compatible with the Solar Fire desktop program, version 6 or higher.

So the difference is breadth and shape. Solar Fire X is the wider program and costs US$360 on Astrolabe's order page. Astro Gold is US$249.99 for both desktop platforms and is the only one of the two with phone and tablet apps, though on Android their own comparison table marks Astro Maps, Professional Reports and iCloud sync as absent. If you are choosing between them, choose on depth against portability.

What neither of them does, on any platform, is hold the people you read for or the consultations you had with them. On 2 November 2026 we read the Astro Gold home page, the macOS, Windows and iOS pages, the cross-platform comparison table and the FAQ, and found no mention of recording a session, transcribing one, or keeping any record of a person beyond a saved chart file. That is not a flaw in either program. It is simply outside what chart software was built to be, which is why swapping one calculator for another calculator never fixes it.

What you give up by switching

Stated plainly, because finding this out in week three is worse than reading it now.

  • Calculation breadth. Six house systems here against more than 25, five ayanamshas against nine sidereal zodiacs, and no astro-mapping, no Vedic dasas and no nakshatras. If your work lives in those, this is not a replacement.
  • Written interpretation reports. Astro Gold sells Professional Reports you can print and hand over. There is no equivalent here, on purpose: the assistant finds facts in the chart and does not write prose about them.
  • An app you install. A browser tool needs a connection, and an installed program is the one that opens when the signal goes. Astro Gold also has store apps for iPhone, iPad and Android; our phone experience is the web app in a phone browser.
  • One wheel design rather than a set, and no chart-art designer.
  • A licence rather than a subscription. US$249.99 is a one-off figure you own. A subscription is a line in your monthly costs and has to earn that line every month.

You do not have to give any of it up at once. Astro Gold stays installed, and nothing about starting a trial here takes it away.

Moving your charts across

Astro Gold saves the same .SFcht chart files Solar Fire uses, and Steliara reads them, so you upload the file directly. Up to 5 MiB and 2,000 records per file, with likely duplicates flagged before anything is saved rather than after.

What comes over. The name on each record, the birth date and local time, the place and its coordinates, the UTC offset stored in the file, and the notes you kept against each chart, which arrive as notes on the chart here. You can send a file into your library as separate charts, or gather a set into one person's profile. The import lives in your account settings and a large file can be paused and finished later.

What you re-enter. Your calculation settings do not travel, so charts are recalculated here with the house system and zodiac you choose during the import; check those before you confirm. Subsidiary charts stored inside a record, such as a progression saved alongside the radix, are not carried over either. The main chart is saved and you rebuild the derived ones in seconds. Anything that was never in a chart file, your notebook and your folder of session documents, is still yours to bring across by hand, and most people bring only the people they are still reading for. Every format Steliara reads, with the limits of each, is in how to move charts between astrology programs.
