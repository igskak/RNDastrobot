Example of a personal month

Example for a fictional person born on 17 May 1990. Your own month is calculated from your birth data.

Your sky in November

  • 3–8 Nov Venus meets your Sun — visibility and self-worth

    Compliments may land more deeply this week, or you may notice the moments when you play yourself down. Pay attention to the clothes, people and choices that make you feel more like yourself — not simply more acceptable.

    Where are you ready to stop asking for permission to enjoy being yourself?

  • 14–19 Nov Mars activates your 4th house — home and belonging

    You may feel an urge to clear a corner, address a tension at home or ask for more room of your own. Rather than changing everything, notice the one place — or relationship — that no longer lets you exhale.

    If your home could support the person you are becoming, what would you change first, and why?

  • 24 Nov Full Moon in your 10th house — work and recognition

    A result, deadline or conversation may make your progress visible. Before rushing to the next goal, notice what you have built — and whether the version of success you are chasing still feels like yours.

    What are you proud to claim as your own, and whose approval are you ready to stop measuring it by?

Your month in your own words

Across seven November entries, three words keep returning: “rest”, “sister” and “project”. Your private life and visible work seem to be asking for attention at the same time.

Near the full moon in your 10th house you wrote: “I want my work to be seen, but not at the cost of coming home depleted.” The same tension appeared in August, when Jupiter crossed the top of your chart.

Carry into December: what would visible success look like if it also left room for your real life?
