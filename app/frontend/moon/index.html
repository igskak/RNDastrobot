    Two ways to stay close to your own rhythm

    The sky is not here to tell you what will happen. It can give you a moment to notice what is already asking for your attention — once at each new and full moon, or as a deeper story unfolding across the whole month.

    Your Moon Letter

    Twice a month, a short letter meets you at a natural turning point. It translates the new or full moon through your own birth chart and gives you a quiet place to hear yourself more clearly.

    • Your sky, in human languageSee which area of life this moon touches and why it may feel more alive now — without jargon or fixed predictions.
    • Questions that open somethingThree carefully chosen prompts help you name what you want, what has shifted and what you are ready to carry forward.
    • A journal that remembersYour answers stay together in a private journal, so a passing feeling can become a pattern you recognise over time.

    Free, twice a month. A few thoughtful minutes that belong only to you.

    Your Personal Month

    $5 a month

    Personal Month gives you the wider view. Instead of meeting the month as a blur of dates, you see its emotional rhythm: the periods that may stir something, the questions worth staying with and the thread connecting it all.

    • Your meaningful datesA personal calendar of the month's strongest transits, translated into situations you may recognise in work, relationships, home and your inner life.
    • Guidance you can actually useEach period comes with a focused reflection — not an instruction — so you can make your own choice with more awareness.
    • Patterns only time can revealAt the end of the month, your entries are reflected back beside the sky of those days, revealing recurring needs, tensions and desires.
    • Your year as a storyOn your birthday, those months become a private yearbook: not a list of predictions, but a portrait of how you changed.

    For anyone who wants not only to know what is happening in the sky, but to understand what it is opening in their life.

    A journal that follows the Moon

    Every month the Moon moves from new to full and back again. Many people use this rhythm for journaling: at the new moon they write down an intention, at the full moon they look back and note what they are grateful for.

    We calculate the exact moment of every new and full moon with the Swiss Ephemeris and find the house of your birth chart where it falls. Each house has its own theme, so your questions change from month to month instead of repeating the same list.

    What this is and what it is not

    These are questions for reflection, not predictions. We do not tell you what will happen; we offer a rhythm, a few questions and an affirmation, and the answers are yours.

    Your answers are saved in your journal so you can find them on any device. We also keep your email and birth data for the letters. You can delete everything at any time with one click.

    Questions

    When is the next new moon?

    Do I need my birth time?

    No. Without a birth time we count the houses from your Sun sign. With your birth time and place we count them from your rising sign, which is more personal.

    What should I write at the new moon?

    One intention in the present tense and one small step for the coming days. Your personal questions depend on the house where the new moon falls in your chart.

    What should I write at the full moon?

    Look back over the last two weeks and name three things you are grateful for. Your letter adds two questions for the house of the full moon.
