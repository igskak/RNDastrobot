Questions

A short explanation of how it works

Is this just a natal chart calculator?

No. The chart is only the beginning. Steliara also supports people profiles, conversation history, notes, recordings, and quick search through astrological data.

What can be stored in a profile?

Charts, notes, conversation recordings, and key moments that help you recover context before the next meeting.

How does the chat work?

Ask a question from chart or forecast data. For example, ask when transiting Pluto is exactly conjunct the natal Moon over the past and next 5 years, and get every exact date without searching by hand.

Will it interpret the chart for me?

No. The point is not to replace your interpretation. The chat helps retrieve data, dates, and links faster.

Can I use professional settings?

Yes. House systems, coordinates, and settings are available for careful astrological work.

Does it help with long-term practice with the same people?

Yes. Profiles, notes, recordings, and key moments help keep the story from getting lost.

What changes before a consultation?

You can remember the person, previous themes, important periods, and current charts faster.

What are calculations based on?

Calculations are based on Swiss Ephemeris and modern astronomical standards.

Do I need to change my style?

No. Steliara should support the way you practice, not impose a ready-made voice.

Where should I begin?

Create the first chart, save a profile, and ask the chat a concrete question about a period or aspect.
