What is cloud astrology software?

Cloud astrology software runs in a web browser instead of being installed on one computer, so your charts and your records live in your account rather than on a single machine. It works the same on macOS and Windows, updates itself, and opens on any device you sign in on.

That description now covers a lot of tools, and they are not interchangeable. Most web based astrology software is a calculator that moved into the browser: you get beautiful wheels, a long list of techniques, and a place to save charts. Steliara is the other kind. The calculation is there, on the Swiss Ephemeris, but the thing it is organised around is the practice: a profile for every person you read for, and the consultation itself recorded, transcribed and summarized onto that profile.

Which one you want depends on whether you are calculating charts or running a practice. The rest of this page is about telling those two apart honestly, including the places where the cheaper tools win.

Is there astrology software that runs properly on a Mac?

Yes, in two ways. Browser-based tools have no operating system requirement at all, so a Mac is just a machine you sign in on. Or you buy a Mac-native program: Astro Gold for macOS is US$249.99, and iPhemeris is a Mac and iOS program with astrocartography built in.

What you cannot easily do is run the old Windows reference program. Astrolabe, who sell Solar Fire, say plainly that they do not officially support it on the Mac, and that customers who manage it use an Intel Mac with Windows and a Windows emulation program, which needs its own Windows licence. Their own guidance adds two things worth knowing before you spend a weekend on it: Solar Fire 9 will not run under Wine, and Mac users have reported that newer Macs with Apple Silicon chips will not run VirtualBox or VMware either.

So on a current Mac the emulator route has quietly closed. If you want that depth on Apple hardware, buy the Mac-native program. If what you actually want is to stop thinking about this, a browser tool never asks the question: the same workspace opens on the Mac in your study, the Windows laptop at a workshop, and a borrowed machine in a hotel, with nothing installed and no licence tied to a computer. We wrote the longer version of this trade, including what you give up, on the Solar Fire alternative page.

Where the other cloud tools are the better buy

A comparison that finds nothing good about the alternatives is marketing, so here is the real version. Two of these are better than us at the thing most people search for.

  • They cost much less. LUNA is $7.99 a month, or $59.99 for a year. AstroApp has a plan that is free for life. Steliara starts at $24 a month and has no free tier at all. If charts are all you need, you should not be paying us three times the price for the half of the product you will not open.
  • They calculate more. LUNA lists 33 house systems; we offer six. AstroApp covers Hellenistic, Medieval, Horary, Vedic, Chinese and financial astrology; we do western natal and transit work. If your technique lives outside that, this is not the tool.

What none of them did, on the pages we read on 2 October 2026, is hold the consultation. That is the whole reason this page exists.

What the cloud actually costs you

Three fair objections to running your practice in a browser. We would rather answer them here than have you find out in week three.

  • There is no offline mode. None. A browser tool needs a connection, so on a train with no signal the installed program is the one that still opens. Plenty of astrologers keep a desktop program for exactly that and use this for everything else, which is a perfectly sensible arrangement.
  • Your content stays yours. Section 5 of the Terms says you retain ownership of what you add: charts, profiles, notes and recordings. We hold a licence to host and show it to you, nothing else, and personal data is never sold.
  • Getting it out is a request, not a button. Being straight about this: there is no one-click export in the app yet. The privacy policy gives you the right to ask for access to your personal data, a portable copy of it, or its deletion, and we act on that. If a subscription lapses the account turns read-only rather than locking you out, so your charts and records stay reachable while you decide.

Recordings have their own clock. Only the audio is ever recorded, never the video, and only after both sides have agreed on the call. The audio is kept for six months and then deleted automatically; the transcript and the summary stay on the person's profile until you delete them.

Questions

Can I use it on a Mac?

Yes. Steliara runs in any browser, the same on a Mac, a Windows machine, a tablet or a borrowed laptop. There is nothing to install and no licence tied to one computer.

Can I run Solar Fire on a Mac instead?

Not with their support. Astrolabe state that they do not officially support Solar Fire on the Mac, that customers who run it there use an Intel Mac with Windows and a Windows emulation program needing its own Windows licence, and that Solar Fire 9 will not run under Wine. Their guidance also reports that newer Macs with Apple Silicon chips will not run VirtualBox or VMware. The Mac-native options are Astro Gold at US$249.99 or iPhemeris.

Does it work offline?

No. It is a cloud application and it needs a connection. If you prep readings somewhere without signal, keep an installed program for that; it is a real gap and we are not going to talk around it.

Who owns the data, and can I get it out?

You keep ownership of everything you add, charts, profiles, notes and recordings, under section 5 of the Terms, and personal data is never sold. You can ask for access to your personal data, a portable copy, or deletion under the privacy policy. There is no one-click export button in the app yet, so for now it is a request to us rather than a download. If a subscription lapses the account goes read-only instead of locking, so nothing vanishes.

What do the cheaper cloud tools do better?

Quite a bit. LUNA is $7.99 a month and lists 33 house systems against our six. AstroApp has a free lifetime plan and covers Hellenistic, Medieval, Vedic, Chinese and financial astrology, where we do western natal and transit work. Do I lose my charts if I switch?

No, and you do not have to retype them. Steliara imports chart files from ZET (.zbs), Astro.com, Solar Fire and Astro Gold (.SFcht) and Astrolog (.as), up to 5 MiB and 2,000 records per file, with likely duplicates flagged before anything is saved. The source program's own calculation settings do not travel, so charts are recalculated here with the house system you pick during the import.
