Is there astrology software that records client sessions?

Yes. Steliara runs the consultation as a video call inside the workspace and records the audio once both sides agree. The recording is transcribed automatically and a short summary is saved on the profile of the person you read for, next to their charts and your notes.

That is the whole point of this page. Charts are table stakes, and so is a list of the people you read for. The part that still evaporates is the hour and a half you actually spent together.

A two-hour reading, and by the next session nobody remembers it

Readings run one to two hours, often longer. Astrologers already tell the people they read for to record it, because anyone listening retains a fraction of what was said. The advice is so common it is practically part of the craft, which tells you the problem is real.

The trouble is who ends up holding the recording. You finish the session, the file sits on their phone, and when they book you again six months later you are the one working from memory. What did we cover. What did they tell me about their mother. Which transit were we watching in March, and did I say spring or autumn. Experienced astrologers forget what they said to whom, not through carelessness but through volume.

So the honest version of the job is not "record the session". It is: the substance of the session should still be here, on the right person's profile, the next time you open it.

What actually happens when you run a session here

  • You open the consultation in Steliara and run it as a video call, with their chart in front of you.
  • You ask to record. They agree in the call, and only then does recording begin. Only the audio is captured.
  • The audio is transcribed for you. There is no file to export and nothing to upload to a second service.
  • A short summary is written from the transcript and saved on that person's profile, beside their charts, your notes and their past sessions.
  • Before the follow-up you open the profile and read what was said, instead of rebuilding it.

Why their recording is not your recording

If the person you read for records on their phone, that solves their problem and not yours. It is worth being precise about the difference:

  • It is their file. You cannot search it, and in practice you will never ask them for a copy.
  • It is raw audio in a queue of voice memos. No transcript, no summary, nothing you can skim in the two minutes before a call.
  • It is attached to nothing. The chart you read from is in one program, your notes are in another, and the session is in their pocket.
  • The person who most needs the history before the next session is you.

Notes by hand, a general recorder, or this

Privacy and consent, plainly

  • Only audio is recorded. Nothing from the video is kept, and the notice in the call says so in those words.
  • Recording does not start until both sides agree. The other person is asked in the call, sees what the recording is for, and can say no.
  • The recording, the transcript and the summary are yours. You keep ownership of everything you add to Steliara, and personal data is not sold.
  • The audio of each consultation is kept for six months, then deleted automatically. The transcript and summary stay on the profile until you delete them or close your account.

Questions

Does Steliara record video, or only audio?

Only audio. The consultation runs as a video call, but nothing from the video is recorded. The consent notice in the call states this plainly: only audio will be recorded, no video.

Does the person I read for have to agree to the recording?

Yes. Recording does not begin until both sides agree inside the call. The person sees what is being asked and can decline, and the session continues unrecorded.

Why is this different from the recording they make on their phone?

That file belongs to them. It is a voice memo with no transcript, no summary and no connection to their chart, and you will not ask for a copy of it. The recording Steliara keeps is yours, searchable, and filed against the right person before the next session.

Do I still need Zoom and a separate transcription tool?

No. The call, the recording, the transcript and the summary all happen in Steliara, so there is no second video app and no separate transcription bill.
