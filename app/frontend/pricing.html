Questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Both plans begin with a free trial so you can set up your workspace and run a few charts before any payment is taken.

Can I switch between monthly and annual?

Anytime. Annual billing is the same product at a lower effective rate — about 17% off the monthly price.

Can I cancel?

Yes, you can cancel at any time from your account settings. You keep access until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

How is payment handled?

Payments and invoicing are securely processed by our reseller, Paddle. Paddle is the merchant of record and handles billing, taxes and receipts.

What currencies do you support?

Prices are listed in USD. Paddle automatically presents the right local currency and tax at checkout.
