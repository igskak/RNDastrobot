What you can calculate

The dasha calculator shows the Vimshottari timeline of life seasons, which starts from the nakshatra your Moon occupied at birth. The Sade Sati calculator shows when Saturn passes through the three signs around your Moon. Kundli matching compares two Moon placements across the eight Ashtakoota areas and gives a score out of 36.

How the calculations are made

Positions come from the Swiss Ephemeris, the astronomical engine behind professional astrology software. The tools use the sidereal zodiac with the Lahiri ayanamsha, the standard in India, and a dasha year of 365.25 days. Your birth place is turned into a time zone so that the local birth time becomes the right moment in world time.

A gentle way to read the results

These tools describe traditional timelines and scores. Treat them as a mirror for reflection and conversation, not as a prediction of events. Where a tradition treats something as challenging, you will see it described as a theme to be aware of, together with a practical step.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The calculators are free and need no account. An optional weekly forecast is available if you would like a personal reading each week.

Do I need my exact birth time?

The Moon moves about thirteen degrees a day, so the time matters. If you do not know it, the tools use midday and tell you when the result could differ.

Which zodiac is used?

The sidereal zodiac with the Lahiri ayanamsha, which is the standard used for kundli in India. It can differ from the sign in a western horoscope.

Where is my data kept?

The free calculators do not store your birth details. If you subscribe, only what is needed to prepare your forecast is kept, and you can delete it from your report page at any time.
