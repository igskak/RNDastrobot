Is there astrology software that records and transcribes consultations?

Yes. Steliara runs the consultation as a video call inside the workspace, records it, produces a transcript, and writes a short summary onto that person's profile. This replaces the usual arrangement of a chart program plus a separate video app plus a separate transcription service.

Astrologers already know sessions need recording: the standard advice to a client is to record on their phone, because they will only remember a fraction of a two-hour reading. What was missing was the recording the astrologer keeps, filed against the right person, searchable later.
