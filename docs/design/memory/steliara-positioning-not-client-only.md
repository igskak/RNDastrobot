Steliara is deliberately positioned for **any practicing astrologer**, not only client-facing/consulting ones — it should feel usable by astrologers who work with family, friends, students, research, or themselves, not just paid clients.

**How to apply (copywriting):** avoid «клиент»/"client" in marketing + UI copy. Use person-oriented language: «человек» / «люди» / «профиль» (the app's actual per-person entity). Chosen 2026-07 during the landing hero rewrite. The hero direction landed on whole-practice framing ("Вся ваша практика — в одном месте", kicker "Рабочее пространство астролога") rather than client-storage framing. See [[design-ux-launch-scope]] and the landing copy draft at docs/plans/landing-copy-rewrite.md.
