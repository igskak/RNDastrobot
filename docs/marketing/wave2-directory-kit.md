## Why this is worth an afternoon

Search Console, first pull, 28 days to 2026-09-14: 105 impressions, average position
19.1. Eight of the top ten queries were not queries at all but misspellings of other
brands — `astrolara`, `skelara`, `steara`, `stelaria`, `stelira`, `stellera`,
`stephilara`, `tislitsara` — at positions 41 to 72.

That is Google telling us it does not know what "Steliara" is. Unqualified `steliara`
autocorrects to **stelara**, Johnson & Johnson's ustekinumab, and the site appears
nowhere on that results page; only the quoted `"steliara"` returns us, at #1.

No amount of content fixes this. An engine learns that a name is a distinct entity by
seeing it asserted consistently across independent sources it already trusts. That is
what this list is for now. Backlinks and discovery are a side effect, not the goal.

**The mechanism is consistency, not volume.** Same name, same URL, same category, same
one-liner, everywhere. Five listings that agree are worth more than fifteen that drift.
Copy from this file rather than retyping, or the drift is guaranteed.

## Tier 1 — entity sources, in this order

These are the listings engines actually consult when deciding what a name refers to.
Roughly an hour for all four.

### 1. LinkedIn Page — the strongest free signal

Create at [linkedin.com/company/setup/new](https://www.linkedin.com/company/setup/new).

Two requirements that stop people: your personal profile must be more than a few days
old with some connections, and **LinkedIn requires a company-domain email** — Gmail and
Outlook are rejected. `steliara.com` has MX records on Spacemail, so set up
`ihor@steliara.com` (or similar) first and verify with that.

Fields: official name `Steliara`, website `https://www.steliara.com`, industry
*Software Development*, size 1-10, type Privately Held, tagline as above, logo 300×300,
banner 1128×191. Fill the About section with the long description.

### 2. Crunchbase

[crunchbase.com](https://www.crunchbase.com) → register → **Contribute Data** → add a
company. Free. Requires a socially-authenticated account. Search "Steliara" first: a
duplicate profile splits the signal and is hard to merge later.

Crunchbase only keeps tech-ecosystem profiles and removes thin ones, so fill the long
description, categories, founded year, website and logo in the first pass rather than
saving a stub.

### 3. Product Hunt

Two distinct things, and the order matters. **Claim the product page now** — that is the
entity record and it is not the launch. **Schedule the actual launch separately**, once
there are a few founding members and two or three testimonials; a launch without momentum
spends a one-time card for nothing.

- Tagline: `The workspace for practicing astrologers`
- Description: Everything about every person you read for in one place: charts, notes,
  and the consultations themselves, recorded, transcribed, and summarized. Works on Mac,
  Windows, any browser. The assistant finds facts in the chart; you stay the interpreter.
- First maker comment: "Hi PH 👋 I built Steliara because practicing astrologers juggle a
  chart tool, a video call, and scattered notes, and the substance of each consultation
  vanishes when the call ends. Steliara keeps it all together and records and summarizes
  the session. The AI never reads the chart for you, it just finds the facts faster. Free
  14-day trial, would love your honest feedback."

### 4. G2 and Capterra — vendor profile now, reviews later

Both let a vendor create a free product profile without any reviews. Do that now: they
are high-authority pages that state plainly what category the product is in, which is
exactly the signal we need, and they rank for "astrology software" style queries on their
own. Reviews come later from the founding cohort and are what makes them convert.

### Not Wikidata

Dropped on 2026-09-18 after checking the policy. An item needs either a sitelink to a
Wikipedia article or "serious and publicly available references". Steliara has neither
yet, so an item would be nominated for deletion and the time is wasted. Revisit if real
press coverage appears.

## How we will know it worked

This is measurable now, which it was not in June. The daily monitor records Search
Console to the `seo-metrics` branch, so:

- **The misspelling queries should fade.** `skelara`, `stephilara` and friends sitting at
  position 41-72 are Google guessing. As the entity firms up, they should drop out of the
  top queries entirely.
- **`steliara` unquoted should start returning the site.** Today it autocorrects to the
  drug and we appear nowhere. Check by hand monthly; it is the single clearest test.
- **Average position should improve for real queries** rather than the current blend.
  `cloud astrology` at 12.3 and `mac astrology software` at 3.0 are the honest signals in
  the current data.

Give it four to eight weeks after the Tier 1 listings are live. Entity resolution is
slower than indexing.
