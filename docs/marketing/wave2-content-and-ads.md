## A. 4-week content calendar (3 posts/week, IG / Telegram / YouTube Shorts)

**Week 1 — The memory problem (capture the consultation)**
1. **Hook:** "You remember about 20 to 30% of a consultation. Your client remembers even less." → so record it. *(IG carousel / Telegram post)*
   - RU hook: «Из консультации вы запоминаете 20-30%. Клиент еще меньше». Поэтому записывайте.
2. **Format:** 20s screen video — a session turning into a transcript and a short summary. Caption: "After the reading, it's already written up. Nothing to retype." *(Shorts/Reels)*
3. **Relatable:** the after-session scramble (typing notes from memory at 11pm). Caption ends with: there's a calmer way.

**Week 2 — Everything in one place**
4. **Hook:** "Where do you keep your clients' charts? And the notes? And the recordings? And the dates that mattered?" *(IG carousel)*
   - RU: «Где у вас карты клиентов? А заметки? А записи? А важные даты?»
5. **Walkthrough:** a person profile — charts + notes + past sessions together. "Before a meeting, it's all already here." *(Short)*
6. **Before/after:** five browser tabs vs one workspace. *(IG single image / Telegram)*

**Week 3 — AI assists, the astrologer interprets (+ accuracy)**
7. **Hook (the trust post):** "AI will never replace the astrologer. Here is what it should actually do." Then: it finds the facts (aspects, exact dates, placements), you interpret. *(IG carousel / Telegram long post / YouTube)*
   - RU: «ИИ никогда не заменит астролога. Вот что он реально должен делать».
8. **Demo:** ask the chart for transit Uranus to natal Venus windows, dates appear in seconds. "You interpret, it digs." *(Short)*
9. **Accuracy:** Swiss Ephemeris + a professional orb setup. Quiet credibility post.

**Week 4 — Founding group + founder**
10. **Founding offer:** "I'm opening Steliara to the first 100 astrologers. Lock today's price before it goes up." *(all channels)*
11. **Founder story:** why I'm building this (short, honest, human). *(Telegram/YouTube)*
12. **Testimonial / call:** once a founding member is happy, share one line + invite the next. *(IG/Telegram)*

**Cadence tip:** film 2-3 short screen demos in one sitting (posts 2, 5, 8), they are your highest-performing format and reusable as ad creative.
