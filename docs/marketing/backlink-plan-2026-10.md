# Backlinks that need a person: where to ask, and what to send

*Written 2026-10-20; **corrected the same day** after the full research (see
[outreach/contacts/README.md](outreach/contacts/README.md)): AFAN's domain now redirects to an
unrelated site, and Gitnux, WorldMetrics and similar "top astrology software" pages are SEO

[wave2-directory-kit.md](wave2-directory-kit.md), which owns
LinkedIn, Crunchbase, G2/Capterra, AlternativeTo, SaaSHub, BetaList and the other
self-serve listings. This file covers what that one does not: **links someone else has to
choose to give us.** Do the kit's Tier 1 first; these build on it.*

## Why this, and why now

Search Console, 28 days to 2026-10-16: 13 clicks, 223 impressions, average position 10.2.
Position went from 19 to 10 in a month on technical fixes and new pages, then stopped.
All five new pages are indexed (checked in Search Console on 2026-10-20) and still get
no impressions, which means indexing is not the limit. Authority is: the domain is
months old and almost nothing on the web points at it. Content can't fix that. A link
from a real astrology organisation, a school or a roundup that already ranks can.

**Rules for everything below**

- **Earned, not bought.** No paid links, no link swaps, no "guest post" farms, no fake
  reviews. Search engines discount these and they put the domain at risk.
- **Say who you are.** Every message and every forum post says it comes from the maker.
- **Ask for the smallest thing.** A listing, a line in a newsletter, a corrected comparison.
  Never "please link to us".
- **Honest about gaps.** Steliara has six house systems, no astrocartography, no Vedic
  dashas and no free tier. Roundup authors and associations will notice; saying it first
  is what makes the message believable.
- **One name, one URL.** `Steliara`, `https://www.steliara.com`. Never spell the drug.
- **Facts to reuse, checked against the live product on 2026-10-20.** Practitioner $24 a
  month, Studio $39 (billed annually $20 and $32). 14-day trial, no card. Recording,
  transcripts and summaries are **Studio only**, audio only, with both sides' consent,
  kept six months. Imports ZET, Solar Fire, Astro Gold, Astrolog files. Swiss Ephemeris
  calculations. Interface in English, German, Russian and Ukrainian.

## 5. Communities and Q&A (slow, and where AI answers learn from)

The three answer pages (`/answers/...`) exist so an engine has something factual to quote.
They only get cited if the same facts appear in places engines already read.

- **Reddit and astrology forums (Skyscript, practitioner groups).** Search for threads
  asking "what software do you use". Answer the actual question first, name three or
  four programs fairly, and only then, if relevant, say "I make Steliara". One honest
  mention a week. **Read each community's self-promotion rule first; if there is a
  rule against it, don't.**
- **Quora.** Answer "What software do professional astrologers use?" and "How do
  astrologers keep client notes?" with the content of the matching answer pages, with the
  source link at the end. Links are nofollow; the value is being cited.
- **Telegram and Facebook groups for practicing astrologers (RU, UK, DE).** Do not post
  links cold. Join, answer, and let people ask. Use the Russian and Ukrainian page
  texts, not a translation of the English post.

> Disclosure line to use every time: *"Full disclosure: I make Steliara, so weigh this
> accordingly."*

**Not to do:** upvote exchanges, sock-puppet accounts, posting the same text in many
places, or pasting this file's emails into forums.

## How we will know it worked

- **Links:** referring domains in Search Console → Links. Today there are almost none.
  Four to six new real domains in two months is a good result for a domain this young.
- **Visits from them:** only DAV and Product Hunt links are counted on the server
  (`/dav`, `?ref=producthunt`). To count another source, ask me to add it to
  `TRACKED_REFS` before sharing the link, or the visit won't be recorded.
- **Position and impressions** from the daily monitor on the `seo-metrics` branch. Give it
  four to eight weeks.
- **The brand test:** unquoted `steliara` should stop autocorrecting to the drug. Check
  by hand monthly.
