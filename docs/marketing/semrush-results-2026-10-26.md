## What this tells us (and what it does not)

1. **Brand queries dwarf "alternative" queries.** Astro Gold's brand is searched about 2,300 a
   month; the queries a switcher would type are in the tens or missing. This is the Astrolium
   lesson again: a competitor page only works if people are leaving that competitor.
2. **Semrush has no data for what our best page ranks for.** Clusters 3, 4 and 5 are n/a, yet
   `/cloud-astrology-software` gets most of our clicks from "cloud astrology" (Search Console).
   Absence from Semrush is not absence of demand for a niche this small. Search Console is the
   better source for the long tail; Semrush is a floor, not a ceiling.
3. **The big prize is "astrology software"** (1,900 a month) but at KD 52 it is not winnable
   by a page; it is a result of authority. That is why links (the outreach work) matter more
   than the next page.
4. **"astrofolio"** (720, KD 13) is worth a ten-minute look by a person: what is it, is it a
   competitor, and who searches it. Not queued, because the demand is for that brand.
