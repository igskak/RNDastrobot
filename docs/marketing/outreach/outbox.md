*Written 2026-10-20. **Nothing has been sent.** Each draft is for you to read, change and send
from your own address, one at a time, to the contact path shown (taken from the
verified files in [contacts/](contacts/)). Before sending: re-open the contact page and check
the address is still current; fill the brackets; read [messaging-kit.md](messaging-kit.md)
for the rules (honesty about gaps, the opt-out line, no promises you haven't set up).*

**Brackets to fill or decide**
- `[Name]`: the person or team, if the page names one. "Hello," is fine when it doesn't.
- `[OFFER]`: your decision. Default wording used below: *"a free working account for as long as
  your test takes, set up by me."* A 90-day trial like DAV's needs a promo code (ask me).
- `[Nachname]`, `[email]`: your surname and your steliara.com address.
- **Strong claim.** The drafts say *built together with practicing astrologers*. Use "made by
  astrologers, for astrologers" only if it is literally true of you or your team.
- **Paid options** (adverts, banners, stands) are not in the drafts. They are listed in the
  contacts files with prices; paying is your decision.

1. **D2 (DAV 2027 talk proposal), deadline 10 Nov.**
