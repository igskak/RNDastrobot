# Outreach messaging kit

*Written 2026-10-20. Texts you send to the organisations and people in
[contacts/README.md](contacts/README.md). Facts match `.agents/product-marketing.md` and the live
site on this date. The earlier plan [../backlink-plan-2026-10.md](../backlink-plan-2026-10.md)

## Read before sending anything

1. **The line that is yours to confirm.** You asked to say that Steliara is *made by
   astrologers for astrologers*. The texts below say **"built together with practicing
   astrologers, and their feedback shapes what we build"**, which matches the maker note
   already used in the directory kit. If the stronger claim is literally true (you or
   someone on the team practises astrology), swap in the *strong version* marked in each
   block. An astrologer who checks and finds a software developer will remember it for a
   long time, so only use it if it holds.
2. **The offers in square brackets are decisions, not facts.** `[OFFER]` defaults to "a
   free working account for the length of your test, set up personally". Today the product
   gives 14 days, and the code `dav2026` gives 90 for DAV (until 30 Nov, 100 uses). Any
   longer trial for other groups needs a code created first (ask me), or a manual account
   that you set up. Do not promise what isn't set up.
3. **One message per organisation, one at a time**, from your own address (ihor@ at
   steliara.com once it exists). Write to the public contact path in the contacts file. Do
   not send to private personal addresses you find elsewhere.
4. **Opt-out line.** Every first message ends with one line saying they can tell you not
   to write again, and you honour it the same day. Keep a record in the tracker.
5. **Nothing is sent from here.** Everything below is a draft for you to read, change and
   send.
6. **Be honest about the gaps.** Six house systems, no astrocartography, no Vedic dashas, no
   free tier, recording is Studio only. Saying it first is what makes the rest credible.

## The facts, in one block (copy from here)

- **What:** a browser workspace for astrologers who work with clients. Charts on Swiss
  Ephemeris. A profile for every person you read for, with their charts and your notes.
  Consultations run as video calls in the app and can be recorded (audio only, both sides
  agree, kept six months), transcribed and summarized onto that profile.
- **For whom:** practising astrologers and students who see clients. Not casual chart
  readers.
- **Price:** Practitioner $24/month, Studio $39/month ($20 and $32 billed annually).
  Recording, transcripts and summaries are Studio only. 14-day trial, no card.
- **Imports:** Solar Fire, Astro Gold, ZET, Astrolog and other chart files.
- **Languages:** English, German, Russian, Ukrainian.
- **Not yet:** astrocartography, Vedic dashas, more than six house systems.
- **Who:** Ihor, founder, in the Czech Republic.

## The positioning in three lengths

**One line.** Steliara is built together with practicing astrologers: charts, the people
you read for, and recorded, summarized consultations in one place.
*Strong version (only if true):* Steliara is made by astrologers, for astrologers.

**Three lines.** Steliara is a browser workspace for astrologers who work with clients.
Charts, notes and every consultation (recorded with consent, transcribed and summarized)
live on each person's profile, so you never rebuild a session from memory. It is young,
and what it becomes depends on feedback from the people who use it.

**The feedback paragraph (use in most messages).** I'm not asking you to switch
anything. I'm looking for a few working astrologers who will try it on one real client
workflow and tell me plainly what's missing or wrong. What I hear decides what I build
next, and I'll tell you what I changed because of it.

## B. Individual teacher, podcaster, YouTuber or blogger: feedback first

Write to the public contact path (a contact form or the business address on their site).
Lead with their work. Don't ask for anything on the first message except a reaction.

**English**

> **Subject:** Your [episode / article / course], and a question from a tool-maker
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm Ihor. I build Steliara, a browser workspace for astrologers who work with clients
> (charts, notes, and consultations that can be recorded with consent, transcribed and
> summarized). I came across [specific thing they made] and [one real, specific reason
> it mattered to you].
>
> I'm not asking you to promote anything. I'd like your honest reaction: you know what
> working astrologers actually need, and I'd rather hear what's wrong than build in the
> dark. [OFFER] If it's not for you, no problem, and I won't follow up more than once.
>
> Thanks for the work you put out.
> Ihor · https://www.steliara.com

*Never write "I loved your [topic]" without naming the thing; a generic compliment reads
as a template and gets deleted.*

**Follow-up (once, 7–10 days later)**

> Hi [Name], one short follow-up on my note from last week. If it isn't relevant, no need
> to reply and I won't write again. Ihor

## E. Replies you will get

| They say | Reply |
|----------|-------|
| "How much is it?" | Practitioner $24/month, Studio $39 (recording is Studio only). 14-day trial, no card. Then your [OFFER] if one applies. |
| "Does it do [astrocartography / Vedic / horary]?" | "Not yet. That's exactly the kind of thing I'd like to know people need. Which would matter most to you?" Then record it. |
| "Is the recording safe?" | Audio only, recorded only when both sides agree, kept six months and then deleted automatically; the transcript and summary stay on the profile until you delete them. |
| "Can I move my charts over?" | Yes: Solar Fire, Astro Gold, ZET, Astrolog and other chart files, up to 2,000 charts per file. Sessions recorded in other tools don't come across. |
| "Not interested" | "Thanks for telling me. I won't write again." Note it in the tracker. |
| "Can we feature you for a fee?" | Say you'll think about it; don't pay for links. Paid placement as a clearly marked sponsor is a different question, and only for events where working astrologers are the audience. |

## F. What to do with the feedback

Keep one list, `feedback-log` in the tracker: who said it, what, how often. Reply to
every person within a week, and **close the loop** when something ships ("you asked for
X; it's live"). That is the part that turns a contact into someone who recommends you.
