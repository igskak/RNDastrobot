# Outbox, Moon: drafts for the sites that publish moon dates

*Written 2026-11-12. **Nothing has been sent.** Every draft is for you to read, change and send
from your own address, one at a time. Same rules as [messaging-kit.md](messaging-kit.md): be
honest, one message per site, an opt-out line, nothing promised that is not set up. The
professional-astrologer drafts are in [outbox.md](outbox.md); this file adds the Moon letters
from [../organic-placement-list-2026-11.md](../organic-placement-list-2026-11.md), section 3,
and the few professional targets that outbox.md does not cover.*

## Read before sending (three things are yours to decide)

1. **Swiss Ephemeris licence.** The data page names the Swiss Ephemeris and Astrodienst. Choose
   AGPL or the Professional Licence **before** these letters go out: a letter that invites
   people to look at the method invites them to look at that too.
2. **A sender address for corrections.** The page sends corrections to "the contact on the terms
   page", which is a personal address today. Use one steliara.com address in the letters, the
   same one as in `[email]`, and put it on the terms page too.
3. **"Free to use with a link back"** is the only usage line on /moon/data. If you want a real
   licence (for example CC BY 4.0), change the page first; the letters say only what the page says.

Also: the non-English drafts were written by me and **not reviewed by a native speaker**. Have
someone read the one you send, or send it in English if the site accepts that.

## The facts every letter uses (all true on 2026-11-12)

- Exact new and full moon instants, November 2026 to December 2027, 29 dates, in UTC, at
  https://www.steliara.com/moon/data, with CSV, JSON and an .ics calendar feed.
- Checked against an independent calculation (JPL DE421 through the Skyfield library) on
  9 November 2026: largest difference **0.6 seconds**. The Steliara dates themselves come from the
  Swiss Ephemeris. We did **not** compare with any almanac or with another site's calendar.
- The page is English only; the data is language-neutral. The calculator, the dated pages and the
  printable 2027 planner exist in en, pl, de, fr, it, es, pt (`/moon/<lang>`, `/moon/<lang>/planner`,
  `/moon/<lang>/lunations.ics`).
- Never claim an error on their side, never ask for a link. The ask is "compare, and tell me if
  we differ by more than a minute". If they find a difference, **look at it and publish the
  outcome on the page**, as the page promises.

**Do not send to:** calendrierlunaire.org (its mailto goes to someone else's address, per the
research); personal addresses found elsewhere. Re-open each contact page and confirm the address
or form is still current.
