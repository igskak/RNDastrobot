# German-speaking market: Germany, Austria, Switzerland

*Researched 2026-10-12 to 2026-10-20. Every URL below was opened and checked for 2025–2026
activity; the last column says what was seen. Contact paths are public contact pages or
role addresses printed on the site. Where an address is hidden or belongs to a named
person, only the page is given. **Nothing here has been contacted.** "Unverified" means
exactly that. Priorities: A = contact first.*

**The DAV is the gate.** Its "befreundete Verbände" page names 16 bodies; the Austrian,
Swiss and several other groups belong to it or are tied to it, so a good first contact
travels. The congress and newsletter are paid for exhibitors and advertisers, with a member
discount; a **speaker slot is free**.
