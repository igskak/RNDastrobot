# Ukraine, Czechia, Slovakia, Poland and the Russian-language diaspora

*Researched 2026-10-20. Every page was opened; the last column says what was seen. Only
institutional addresses and contact pages are recorded, never personal emails or phones of
individual teachers. **Nothing here has been contacted.** Priorities: A = contact first.*

**Market rule applied.** Russia and Russian-registered organisations are excluded (list at
the bottom). Russian-language targets are only those based elsewhere. **Result: 25 verified
entries, fewer than hoped.** Most Ukrainian "associations" are dead, and no Slovak
association exists to contact. Schools are the working channel: they have graduate clubs and
Telegram groups, which is exactly where honest feedback comes from.
