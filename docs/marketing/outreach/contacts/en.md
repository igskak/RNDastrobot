# English-speaking astrology profession (US, UK, Australia, international)

*Researched 2026-10-20. Every page was opened unless a note says otherwise; the last column
says what was seen. Only organisation or role addresses printed on public pages are
recorded; where the only printed address was personal, the page is given instead.
**Nothing here has been contacted.** Priorities: A = contact first.*

**What changed since the first backlink plan.** AFAN's domain now redirects to an unrelated
site, so it is **dropped**. Gitnux, WorldMetrics, ZipDo and WifiTalents "top astrology
software" lists turned out to be SEO aggregators with no author and no astrology
credentials: low value, skip them. The roundup that is worth a look is the one practitioner
resource found (The Astrology Podcast's software-review playlist, row 30).

**Where to start:** OPA (a body for the *business* of practice, feedback-friendly), ISAR
(its ethics code and certification cover consulting, the same ground as recorded sessions),
the AA, Astrology University, Kepler College, The Astrology Podcast and Starzology.

## Notes

- **Timing.** UAC26 and the AA conference have just ended. NORWAC is 27–31 May 2027. The
  Evolving Astrologer's next deadline is **25 Nov**.
- **Paid-only routes.** *Stellar* (£75–290), the AA conference programme, *The Evolving
  Astrologer* ($40–220) and Astrology Weekly's business tiers have public rate cards. No
  free listing or editorial route was found for the AA, OPA, TMA or AFA magazines.
  **Paying is your decision.**
- **Membership.** AA from £37/yr, AFA $45–60/yr; NCGR, ISAR and OPA fees were not captured.
