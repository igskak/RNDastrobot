## How it adds up

**37 priority-A targets**, 56 B, 42 C. The strongest by far is the **DAV**: the other German-language
bodies are tied to it, so one good introduction travels. The most useful for honest feedback from
working professionals are **OPA** (the business of practice), **ISAR** (ethics of consulting, which
touches our recording feature), and the **schools** (Astrology University, Kepler, Astropraxis,
Online Akademie Astrologie, the Ukrainian schools), which have graduate clubs and chat groups.

What the research could **not** find, so you can plan around it:
- **No free editorial routes** into the AA, OPA, TMA or AFA magazines: paid adverts or a
  personal introduction. The cheapest paid options are listed with prices.
- **No active Ukrainian or Slovak association.** Schools are the channel there.
- **No independent software reviewers** in German, Czech or Ukrainian; in English only Hank
  Friedman, TMA and one podcast playlist.
- **Reddit, Facebook, Skyscript and several directories** blocked the research tool: those rules
  are second-hand and must be read by you first.

## Rules for sending (short)

- Read the draft, fill the brackets, check the contact page is still current.
- One organisation, one message, then **one** follow-up after 7–10 days; then stop.
- Every first message ends with the opt-out line. If someone says stop, write it in the tracker
  and never write again.
- Offer only what exists. A longer trial needs a promo code first.
- Paid placements are your decision; the files list the prices.
- **Before publicity: check the Swiss Ephemeris licence** (see the end of
  [directories-communities.md](directories-communities.md)).
