# Micro-influencer campaign — ready-to-run playbook

*Created 2026-07-15. Replaces the €200–300/mo Google Ads spend with peer-driven micro-blogger promotion.*
*Voice: soft, warm, human. Founder-to-peer, never brand-to-influencer. No "clients/CRM", no AI/business jargon in anything the audience sees. See [.agents/product-marketing.md](../../.agents/product-marketing.md) and [wave1-outreach-and-emails.md](wave1-outreach-and-emails.md) (section D is the seed for this).*

---

## 0. Why this beats the Google Ads spend

Google Search pulled B2C junk (see the campaign audit): broad intent, wrong audience, ~€16/signup, zero trust. Steliara is a **tool for practicing astrologers**, so the highest-signal place to be recommended is inside the feed of an astrologer your buyers already trust. A micro-blogger deal gives us:

- **Precise audience** — an astrology teacher's followers *are* practicing/aspiring astrologers, not horoscope consumers.
- **Trust** — a peer showing the tool in their real workflow outconverts any ad.
- **An evergreen asset** — a good post/video keeps sending signups for months at no extra cost.
- **A second win** — every partner is themselves a potential paying user (they're exactly our ICP).

The catch that kills most influencer money: **wrong-shaped audience.** A 200k "daily horoscope" meme page is worth *less* than a 4k channel of astrology students. Engagement and audience-fit beat reach every time. Section 2 is the filter.

## 1. The market (two tracks — NOT Russia)

Per positioning: Ukrainian + English-speaking (international/diaspora) astrologers. RU is a UI language and reaches diaspora practitioners, **not the Russia market** (explicit anti-persona).

| Track | Who | Where they gather | Language of the deal |
|-------|-----|-------------------|----------------------|
| **A. UA + RU-language diaspora** | Practicing astrologers, astrology-school teachers, course sellers, mentors | **Telegram** (channels + practitioner chats — strongest here), Instagram, YouTube | UK / RU |
| **B. English / international** | Modern-astrology creators, teachers, consultation-based astrologers | Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Substack/newsletters, astrology podcasts | EN |

Start with **Track A** (competitor-thin for this workspace category — Chronos does calc only, Astrolium isn't localized). Run Track B in parallel at lower volume because English rates are higher.

## 2. Blogger persona ("портрет") + vetting

### The person we want
A **practicing astrologer or teacher whose audience is other astrologers / serious students** — someone who does real consultations, teaches, or sells courses, and talks about their *practice* (not just posts horoscopes).

- **Size:** nano **1k–10k** and micro **10k–50k**. Sweet spot ~**3k–25k** with live engagement.
- **Engagement over reach:** want ≥3–5% engagement, a comment section full of peer astrologers, real questions about technique.
- **Green flags:** teaches/mentors, mentions doing consultations, shows chart screenshots, sells a course or webinars, complains about juggling tools/notes, audience asks "what software do you use?"
- **Format fit:** already does screen-shares, tutorials, "my workflow" content — the tool slots in naturally.

### Hard filters — skip if any is true
- Audience = end consumers wanting *their own* horoscope read (they will never buy a practitioner tool).
- Pure entertainment / meme / "daily sign" page, or dead engagement behind a big number.
- **Russia-based or Russia-market focused** (anti-persona — do not engage).
- Follower count bought (spikes, generic emoji comments, no astrologer-peers).

### 60-second vetting checklist (before any outreach)
1. Do they mention **doing consultations / teaching**, not just posting content? ✅
2. Are commenters **other astrologers/students** (asking technique) vs random fans? ✅
3. Any post where they show **chart software or their workflow**? ✅ (perfect hook)
4. Engagement rate ≥3% and comments look human? ✅
5. Not Russia-market, reachable in EN/UK/RU? ✅
→ 4–5 yeses = A-tier target. 2–3 = barter/affiliate only. <2 = skip.

## 3. The offer — how to make it feel natural

The whole design principle: **it's not an ad buy, it's giving a fellow astrologer a tool and letting them tell their people if it earns its place.** Naturalness comes from sequence and freedom, not from clever copy.

**Four rules that keep it authentic:**
1. **Give real access first, no strings.** Full Pro, free, before any talk of a post. Ask them to actually run one consultation through it. Their honest reaction is the product.
2. **Founder-to-peer, not brand-to-creator.** You built this for astrologers like them. That's true, so say it plainly.
3. **Let them use their own voice.** A loose brief (things worth showing), never a script. "Here's how I keep my people's history now" beats read-aloud ad copy.
4. **Make the post a gift to their audience.** Their followers get a real perk (extended trial / first month), so the post is a favor, not a sell. And disclose the partnership honestly — trust is the currency in this niche.

### The 3-layer deal ladder (offer the lightest that fits)

| Layer | What the partner gets | What we ask | Cash cost |
|-------|-----------------------|-------------|-----------|
| **L1 — Barter + affiliate (backbone, unlimited)** | Free **Pro for a year** (or founding price-lock for life) + personal affiliate code + a real perk for their audience (30-day trial vs 14, or first month free) | Honest feedback; *if* they like it, one organic mention + share the code. No obligation. | **$0** + revenue share |
| **L2 — Anchor paid integration (2–3/mo)** | Everything in L1 **plus a flat fee** for one dedicated piece (a "how I run my practice" post / story set / short video showing the tool) | One agreed deliverable, their voice, honest disclosure | **$50–100** each |
| **L3 — Performance bonus (aligns incentives)** | Bonus on top for partners whose code actually converts | Nothing extra — just rewards results | **$20–30** per bonus tier |

**Affiliate economics (L1 backbone):** offer **30% recurring for 12 months** *or* a flat **$12 per paid conversion** (partner picks). At $24–39/mo that's ~$85–140 per customer over a year — generous enough to be worth posting about, cheap next to €16/signup ads, and it only pays out on real revenue.

## 4. Budget — €250/mo worked example (~$270)

The product's marginal cost is near zero, so **free access is the main currency** and cash is reserved for the few placements most likely to convert.

| Line | Spend | Buys |
|------|-------|------|
| Anchor paid integrations (L2) | **~$180** | 2–3 dedicated posts/videos from best-fit teachers |
| Performance bonus pool (L3) | **~$70** | 2–3 bonuses to partners whose codes convert |
| Barter + affiliate (L1) | **$0 upfront** | Unlimited partners; pays only on real signups |
| **Total** | **~$250** | |

**Sequencing:** Month 1 — spend the anchor cash on 2 well-vetted A-tier teachers, sign 8–12 L1 barter partners. Month 2+ — double down on whoever's code actually converts (move them to L2, pay the bonus), quietly drop dead partners. Let performance, not follower count, decide where next month's cash goes.

**Rough rate sanity-check (per dedicated placement):**
- UA / RU-diaspora micro (3k–25k): **$30–100**, many will do barter+affiliate only.
- English micro (5k–30k): **$75–250** — do fewer, lean harder on affiliate here.
- Rule: never pay more than ~$100 flat until a partner has *proven* they convert. Then bonus generously.

## 5. Outreach — the sequence

Send **1:1, personal, edited to the specific person.** First line must prove you actually watched their content. Never a link dump. Always offer to set up their account for them.

### Step 1 — Warm up (before you DM)
Follow, genuinely engage with 1–2 recent posts over a few days. Cold-cold DMs to creators mostly die.

### Step 2 — First message (lead with free access, not a deal)

**EN**
> Hi [name], I've been following how you [specific: teach synastry / walk through a chart on Tuesdays]. I built Steliara, a workspace where everything about every person you read for lives in one place: their charts, your notes, and the actual consultations recorded, transcribed and summarized, so you never rebuild the context from memory before a session. I'd genuinely just love you to have it, full access, free, no strings. If it earns a place in your practice we can talk about your audience later. Can I set up your account so you can try it on a real reading this week?

**RU**
> Здравствуйте, [имя]. Слежу за тем, как вы [конкретно: разбираете синастрию / по вторникам ведёте разбор карты]. Я сделал Steliara — пространство, где всё о каждом человеке, которого вы консультируете, лежит в одном месте: его карты, ваши заметки и сами консультации, записанные, расшифрованные и со сводкой. Не нужно перед встречей восстанавливать контекст по памяти. Хочу просто подарить вам полный доступ, бесплатно, без условий. Если инструмент займёт место в вашей практике — про аудиторию поговорим потом. Можно настрою вам аккаунт, чтобы вы попробовали на реальной консультации на этой неделе?

**UK**
> Вітаю, [імʼя]. Стежу за тим, як ви [конкретно: розбираєте синастрію / по вівторках ведете розбір карти]. Я зробив Steliara — простір, де все про кожну людину, яку ви консультуєте, лежить в одному місці: її карти, ваші нотатки і самі консультації, записані, розшифровані та зі зведенням. Не треба перед зустріччю відновлювати контекст з памʼяті. Хочу просто подарувати вам повний доступ, безкоштовно, без умов. Якщо інструмент займе місце у вашій практиці — про аудиторію поговоримо потім. Можна налаштую вам акаунт, щоб ви спробували на реальній консультації цього тижня?

### Step 3 — After they've tried it (introduce the collab, only now)

**EN**
> So glad it clicked for you. If it genuinely helps your work, I'd love to set you up as a partner: your own code that gives your audience an extended trial, and you earn 30% for a year on anyone who stays (or a flat amount per person, your call). Totally your voice, no script, only if you mean it. Want me to send the code and a couple of things worth showing?

**RU**
> Рад, что откликнулось. Если инструмент правда помогает в работе, предложу партнёрство: ваш личный код даёт вашей аудитории продлённый доступ, а вы получаете 30% в течение года с каждого, кто останется (или фикс за человека — как удобнее). Полностью вашими словами, без сценария, только если искренне. Прислать код и пару вещей, которые стоит показать?

**UK**
> Радий, що відгукнулося. Якщо інструмент справді допомагає в роботі, запропоную партнерство: ваш особистий код дає вашій аудиторії подовжений доступ, а ви отримуєте 30% протягом року з кожного, хто залишиться (або фікс за людину — як зручніше). Повністю вашими словами, без сценарію, лише якщо щиро. Надіслати код і кілька речей, які варто показати?

### Step 4 — Paid ask (only for A-tier who fit an anchor slot)
Same message + one line: *"If you'd be up for one dedicated post about how you run your practice, I can also cover it — [amount] — on top of the affiliate. Whatever feels honest to your audience."*

### Follow-ups (reuse wave1 cadence)
- **+3 days:** one specific value nudge (*"the part people love most: after a reading it's already recorded and summarized, nothing to retype"*).
- **+1 week:** final, gracious, no pressure. Then stop.

## 6. Partner brief (send *after* they say yes — a guide, not a script)

> **Thank you.** Please use your own voice — this only works if it's honestly yours. A few things your audience tends to find most useful to see:
> - Open a real person's profile: charts + your notes + past sessions in one place.
> - Show a consultation being recorded → transcript → summary (the thing nothing else does).
> - One honest sentence on what changed in your workflow.
> - Your code **[CODE]** gives them [30-day trial / first month]; you earn on anyone who stays.
> - Please mark it as a partnership (#ad / "партнёрский") — your audience's trust matters more than any post.
> No deadline pressure. Post when it feels right.

## 7. Tracking — one sheet, five things that matter

Create a Google Sheet `partners` with columns:

`Handle | Track (A/B) | Followers | Eng% | Fit (A/B/C) | Stage (contacted / trying / partner / posted / dropped) | Deal (L1/L2/L3) | Code | Cash paid | Trials from code | Paid from code | $ earned by them | Notes`

- **One unique code per partner** = clean attribution (also confirms the signup-gclid / server-side attribution path is capturing source; if codes convert but don't show up, that's the known attribution bug — flag it).
- Review weekly. **Cash follows conversions, not followers.**

### KPIs (what "working" looks like)
| Metric | Month-1 target | Read |
|--------|---------------|------|
| Partners signed (L1+L2) | 10–15 | Enough at-bats |
| Trials from codes | 30–60 | Reach × fit working |
| Trial→paid | ≥15% | Matches product-marketing funnel target |
| Blended cost / paid signup | **< €10** | Beat the ~€16 Google Ads number |
| Partners who *themselves* convert | ≥2 | Bonus signal — ICP validation |

## 8. 30-day plan

- **Days 1–3:** Build the target list. Aim for **30–40 vetted handles** (25 Track A, 10–15 Track B) in the sheet, scored by fit.
- **Days 3–4:** Warm up (follow + engage) the top 20.
- **Days 5–10:** Send Step-2 messages to the top 20, 1:1 and personalized. Set up accounts fast for anyone who says yes.
- **Days 10–20:** Onboard triers, gather feedback, convert warm ones to L1 partners, book 2 anchor (L2) placements with the best fit.
- **Days 20–30:** First posts go live. Watch codes. Pay bonuses to converters. Refill the top of the list.
- **Month 2:** Reallocate all cash toward proven converters; expand Track B if a partner there converts.

### Sourcing where to find them
- **Telegram:** search astrology-school channels + their linked practitioner chats; look at who the teachers themselves recommend.
- **Instagram/YouTube:** search hashtags/terms astrologers (not consumers) use — natal chart teaching, synastry, transits, astrology course; check who a good creator follows/collabs with.
- **Schools as a multiplier:** every astrology school (see wave1 section C) has graduate-teachers with their own small audiences — one school intro can surface several fit partners.
- **Snowball:** ask every happy partner "who else teaches like you?" — best-fit leads come from inside the niche.
